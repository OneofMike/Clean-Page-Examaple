BEFORE YOUR CLEAN

A few helpful details

9 quick details ✦
💬 Quote-based pricing
Final pricing depends on home size, condition, service type, requested add-ons, and the details you provide.
📅 Rescheduling
Please give as much notice as possible if you need to move your cleaning to a different day or time.
🔑 Home access
Make sure we have a clear way to enter the property at the scheduled time, whether you are home or providing access instructions.
🚫 Cancellations
Last-minute cancellations may be subject to a fee when a time slot has been reserved specifically for your home.
🐾 Pets
Pets are welcome. Just let us know ahead of time and secure any pet that may be nervous around new people or cleaning equipment.
🧴 Cleaning supplies
Standard supplies can be provided. If your home requires a specific product or you prefer certain products, include that in your request.
💵 Payments
Payment details are confirmed with your quote so you know exactly what is due and when before service begins.
＋ Add-ons
Fridge interiors, ovens, windows, laundry, dishes, organization, and other extras should be requested in advance whenever possible.
✨ After your clean
If there is an area you want us to take another look at, reach out promptly so we can review the concern with you.
